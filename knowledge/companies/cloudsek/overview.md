# CloudSEK Research
- **Industry**: Cybersecurity, AI-driven Threat Intelligence, Digital Risk Protection (DRP).
- **Flagship Product**: **XVigil** (detects data leaks, credential theft, and brand threats).
- **Core Value Prop**: "Outside-in" security approach. Monitoring the dark web, deep web, and surface web.
- **Tech Stack Keywords**: Python, Go, Machine Learning (for pattern detection), Elasticsearch, Big Data, Scalable Scrapers.
- **Company Culture**: Fast-paced, innovation-centric, heavy focus on research and AI.
- **Key Challenges**: Processing massive amounts of unstructured data from the dark web in real-time. False positive reduction in AI models.
- **Strategic Angle**: Emphasize experience in **scalable backends**, **data processing pipelines**, and **security mindset**. They value engineers who understand how to handle large volumes of data while maintaining high precision.
